Form filling guidelinesAI/OPSTRG/007T · REV-00
  1. If any Competency is graded as ‘1’ the session shall be graded as UNSATISFACTORY.
  2. More than three “2’s” shall make the overall outcome of the session UNSATISFACTORY.
  3. If, due to the trainees’ performance, the exercises in the session syllabus are not completed, the session shall be assessed as UNSATISFACTORY.
  4. Tick the Applicable OB’s and subsequently the Observed OB’s. Areas where motivational or developmental feedback is required may be highlighted in the space provided for overall remarks.
  5. To assist with the facilitated debrief and to match overall competency grading, reports should capture objective session observations contributing to the overall outcome and grading.
  6. Completed and duly filled forms to submitted as per the shared communication protocols.
  7. For Grading, Pilot Competencies, IECs & Observable Behaviours, refer to the CBTA Manual.
  8. For Grades 1, 2 & 5 when graded need remarks to identify areas that may need refinement/development and/or what went extremely well, as the case may be.
  9. For “How Many” (Y/X %), Grade 1 = 0 to 59% , 2 = 60% to 74% , 3 = 75% to 89% , 4 = 90% to 94% , 5 = 95% to 100% , (Round down to the nearest whole number).
  10. The use of NO / NA should be avoided, however, may be used where the concerned Observable Behaviour and/or Competency was “Not Observed” or “Not Applicable”.
